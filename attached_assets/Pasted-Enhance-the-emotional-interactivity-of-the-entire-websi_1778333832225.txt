Enhance the emotional interactivity of the entire website UI.

IMPORTANT:
The entire application should dynamically change its background colors, lighting effects, particles, gradients, animations, and ambient music based on the user's selected mood.

At the beginning, there should ONLY be 3 mood options:

1. 😊 Happy
2. 😐 Neutral
3. 😔 Sad

---

## GLOBAL MOOD-BASED EXPERIENCE

When a user selects a mood, the ENTIRE WEBSITE EXPERIENCE should adapt emotionally.

This includes:

* background gradients
* floating particles
* lighting/glow effects
* animations
* transitions
* card colors
* button colors
* hover effects
* music
* UI atmosphere

The transition between moods should feel smooth, immersive, and emotionally intelligent.

Use Framer Motion animations and smooth gradient transitions throughout the app.

---

Energetic, uplifting, positive, warm, motivating.

Background:

* Bright animated gradients
* Yellow, orange, gold, warm pink tones
* Moving soft light streaks
* Floating glowing particles

Effects:

* Gentle floating animations
* Dynamic glowing cards
* Animated gradient blobs
* Smooth energetic transitions

Music:

* Automatically start uplifting lo-fi/chill happy music
* Music should autoplay as soon as the page redirects
* Include a floating music control widget

UI Feel:

* Vibrant
* Motivating
* Warm
* Positive energy
